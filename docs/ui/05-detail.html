Pie With Spoons
Told Nov 10 · 45 words
Mom made meatloaf most weeknights. On Saturdays she made pie, and I helped roll the dough. I made my first one by myself when I was maybe ten. It fell apart before it got to the table. Mom laughed, and we ate it with spoons.
