# UI sample copy v1 (for high-fidelity mockups)
EXAMPLE CONTENT ONLY. The elder "Walt" (b. 1948, Dayton, Ohio) is fictional. Every detail in the story below is traceable to the sample chat, so it's safe to show as "what the writing model is allowed to produce."

## Home — greeting + topic cards (childhood recommend order)
- Greeting: "Good morning, Walt."
- Sub: "Pick up where you like. There's no wrong place to start."
- Card 1: **The house you grew up in** — "What do you see when you walk in the front door?"
- Card 2: **Food at home** — "What was on the table on a normal weeknight?"
- Card 3: **Mom and Dad** — "What did your dad do for work?"
- Continue card: **Food at home** — "Last time: Saturday pies with your mom."

## Telling page — sample exchange (text)
AI: What was on the table on a normal weeknight?
Walt: meatloaf mostly. mom made pie on saturdays
AI: Pie on Saturdays. Did you help, or just wait for it?
Walt: I helped roll the dough. made my first one alone when I was maybe 10
AI: How did that first one turn out?
Walt: fell apart before it got to the table. mom laughed and we ate it with spoons
AI: That sounds like a good day. Want to keep going, or save this one for now?

Buttons: "Send" · "Change topic" · "That's enough for today"
Refusal example — Walt: "I'd rather not talk about my dad." AI: "Of course. Let's stay with the kitchen."
